Our Story

From Kerala's Heart to Your Kitchen

MALAYALEES was born from a desire to bring the authentic, time-honoured taste of Kerala coconut oil to kitchens across India.

For generations, families in Kerala have relied on pure coconut oil — not just as a cooking medium, but as a way of life. Its rich aroma filling every home, its golden warmth transforming the simplest ingredient into something extraordinary.

We started MALAYALEES to honour that tradition. To ensure that no matter where you are, you experience the real taste of Kerala — pure, natural and full of flavour.
